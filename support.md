## Frequently asked questions

### How do I cancel Charge Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. In Charge, open **Settings → Manage Subscriptions**, or go to **iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Charge** and tap **Cancel Subscription**. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

### How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID you bought Pro with, then open Charge → **Settings → Restore Purchases**.

### How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>, sign in with your Apple ID, find the Charge purchase and request a refund.

### Can Charge cancel or pay my other subscriptions?

No. Charge only tracks what you enter and reminds you before payments. To cancel a subscription, contact that service (or cancel it in your App Store subscriptions if you pay through Apple).

### I'm not getting reminders

1. **iOS Settings → Charge → Notifications** must be allowed.
2. Check that a **Focus** mode isn't silencing Charge.
3. Make sure the subscription is **active** (or a trial) and its next payment date is correct.
4. Open Charge from time to time — that keeps reminders scheduled well into the future, even if you track many subscriptions.

Reminders arrive at **10 am** local time, the number of days before the payment you chose, plus a heads-up the day before a free trial turns paid.

### How do I sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Use the same Apple ID on each device with **iCloud** turned on. Charge syncs through your private iCloud database automatically; it can take a moment after you make a change.

### I forgot my app-lock PIN

The PIN can't be recovered — it's never stored, only a one-way hash of it. Delete Charge and install it again. If iCloud sync was on, your subscriptions come back from iCloud; set a new PIN in **Settings → App Lock**.

### Why didn't my amounts change when I switched currency?

Charge shows every amount in the one currency you choose. Switching changes the symbol and number format only — **nothing is converted**. Edit amounts if you track subscriptions in a different currency.

### How do free trials work in Charge?

When adding a subscription, choose **Free trial: 7, 14 or 30 days**. It's listed under **Trials**, you get a reminder the day before it turns paid, and it moves to **Active** automatically when the trial ends. Cancel it in Charge if you cancelled it with the provider.

### How do I delete my data?

- One subscription: move it to **Trash**, then **Delete forever**.
- Everything on a device: delete the app.
- Data synced to iCloud: **iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Charge → Delete Data**.

### Can I export my data?

Not yet. If this matters to you, let us know.
